About Me

I'm Aarsh

Senior Solutions Architect (Detections)| SOC

Aarsh Jawa is a Cybersecurity Solutions Architect, researcher, and author dedicated to engineering advanced threat detection and defense mechanisms. Currently leading detection architecture initiatives at Doppel, his work sits at the intersection of threat intelligence, automation, and security operations center (SOC) innovation. Throughout his career, Aarsh has focused on identifying emerging cyber threats, analyzing complex malware campaigns, and translating technical threat data into actionable defense strategies.

Aarsh strongly believes that our actions define who we are and uses his knowledge to help people understand and protect themselves in the digital world. Beyond engineering, he is passionate about making cybersecurity knowledge accessible to everyone. He regularly shares his findings through major technical publications and authored the foundational guide, Jumping into CyberSecurity, to guide readers through ever-changing cyber threats and provide them with the information and techniques to keep their online identities safe.

As a Certified Cyber Crime Intervention Officer, Aarsh also works diligently to assist victims of Cyber Crime, providing critical support and aiding them in the reporting process.

Besides his professional achievements, Aarsh loves reading and traveling. Stay connected with Aarsh on social media to delve deeper into the world of CyberSecurity, gain exclusive content, and receive updates on his future endeavors. Together, let's redefine the boundaries of CyberSecurity and forge a path towards a safer and more secure digital world.
